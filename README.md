# Bhabhi Play

Build an online Bhabhi (Thulla / Getaway) card game. Create an interactive digital card table with 4 players (1 human player and 3 AI bots), implementing the full rule engine: standard 52-card deck dealt evenly, Ace of Spades leading the first trick, mandatory suit-following, the 'Thulla' penalty mechanic when a player cannot follow suit (highest card of lead suit picks up all cards in trick and leads next), fanned player hand with playable card highlighting, turn indicators, trick discard pile, and getaway/win detection. Include sound/visual feedback and quick-play bot mode.
