# Card table visual refresh

## Changes
- Remove the visible “Shuffling the deck…” startup message so the opening appears cleanly.
- Redesign playing cards with larger proportions, clearer corner values, suit pips, stronger face/back detail, and more realistic depth.
- Expand and refine the felt table, rim, trick area, opponent stacks, and player hand spacing across phone and desktop sizes.
- Choose three different bot names from a larger name pool for every new practice deal.

## Validation
- Check the lobby and a bot match at mobile and desktop sizes.
- Confirm cards remain playable, labels fit, bot names change after a new deal, and no browser errors appear.
